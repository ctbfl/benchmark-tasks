# Assets and licenses

The pan, heater, steak, spatula, holder and procedural geometry were created for
this task and are covered by its MIT license. Product photographs guided their
shape only; the photographs are not redistributed or used as textures.

`assets/steak-atlas.png` is an original image generated with OpenAI's built-in
image generation tool. Its left and right panels depict raw marbled beef and a
pan-seared surface. Both appearances use the same steak geometry and rigid body;
only the original bottom face's material binding changes. The texture is included
with the task for use and redistribution under the task license.

The timer and reusable execution/recording scaffolding derive from the MIT-licensed
Read thermometer task. Its notice is retained in `assets/TIMER-LICENSE.txt`.
The timer's font-outline lettering uses DejaVu Sans; its license is retained in
`assets/DejaVu-LICENSE.txt`. The timer automatically displays elapsed simulation
time; its existing button geometry is not a cooking-task control.

The YAM arms, workstation and calibrated cameras are supplied by yam-arena and
referenced through that dependency, not copied into this package. The heater is
represented by rigid geometry and an illuminated indicator; thermal transfer,
food deformation, smoke and internal doneness are not simulated.

Geometry helpers and planning utilities also derive from the MIT-licensed
Seasoning Sprinkling task; its notices are retained in `assets/HELPERS-LICENSE.txt`.
