# Asset sources and licenses

- `assets/tray.usda` and `assets/tile_1.usda` through `tile_8.usda` are original procedural assets generated by `model.py`. Each tile has a 70 mm square foot and a 42 mm square raised face, leaving a 14 mm recessed perimeter. The tray is fixed; the eight tiles are free rigid bodies.
- `assets/textures/1.png` through `8.png` are original digit textures generated using DejaVu Sans Bold. The font is not bundled; its permissive license and required notice are included in `licenses/DejaVu.txt`. Regeneration uses the Debian/Ubuntu `fonts-dejavu-core` package.
- Workstation geometry, YAM robot assets and camera calibration are loaded from the installed yam-arena package (MIT); those assets are not duplicated here. Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab retain their upstream licensing and installation requirements.
- Task source, action recording and original geometric assets are distributed under `LICENSE` (MIT). No downloaded product model or noncommercial-only asset is used.
