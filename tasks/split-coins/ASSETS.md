# Assets and simulation

The four task USD files are original procedural geometry generated by
`build_assets.py`. Asset licenses, attribution and pinned upstream sources are
collected in [LICENSE](LICENSE): the original models are CC BY 4.0; YAM robot
assets and upstream calibration are MIT. No photographs, trademarks or downloaded
meshes are distributed in the task's `assets/` directory.

- `quarter.usda`: silver coin, 24.26 mm diameter, 1.75 mm thick, 5.67 g. Five instances.
- `penny.usda`: copper coin, 19.05 mm diameter, 1.52 mm thick, 2.5 g. Five instances.
  Coin sizes and masses follow US Mint specifications; each collider is a 32-sided
  convex prism with thin visual rim and relief details.
- `jar.usda`: open blue plastic jar without a lid, 68 mm outer and 60 mm inner diameter,
  90 mm tall, 4 mm floor and 120 g. Separate convex wall sections preserve the cavity.
- `tape.usda`: red paper tape, 12 mm wide, marking a 160 x 110 mm frame on each side.
  The thin visual tape does not create a physical barrier.

Coin friction (0.35 static) and jar friction (0.4 static) are simulation approximations.
Arena enables contact reporting on its rigid objects, which sets the PhysX sleep threshold
to zero; the task restores the native PhysX threshold for the coins and jar so a resting
pile settles instead of exchanging small solver impulses. Robot parameters retain the
upstream native settings, and no grasp attachments or table anchors are used.

The standard workstation table, floor and camera stand use runtime-generated
boxes and capsules. Their source is listed in [LICENSE](LICENSE) alongside the
robot assets and calibration.
