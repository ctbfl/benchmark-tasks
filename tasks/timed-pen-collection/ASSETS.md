# Assets and simulation

The four task USD files are original procedural geometry generated by
`build_assets.py`, licensed with this task under the MIT license. Reference product
photographs guided the shapes; no photographs, trademarks or downloaded product
meshes are distributed.

- `pen.usda`: 150 mm rigid pen, maximum barrel radius 5.2 mm, 12 g, with a narrow tip,
  dark grip, rear cap and metal clip. Five instances share this asset.
- `cup.usda`: white rounded rectangular holder, 78.74 × 73.66 × 104.14 mm,
  69.85 × 63.50 mm opening, 6 mm floor and 280 g mass. Separate convex wall sections
  preserve the cavity; the gray floor supports the pens.
- `timer.usda`: 180 × 70 mm footprint, 86 mm housing height and 700 g free base.
  Four rubber feet rest on the table. The 25 g top button has 6 mm travel and a
  passive 500 N/m, 4 N s/m return spring. A downward crossing of 3 mm toggles timing;
  returning above 1 mm rearms the switch. The display is MM:SS:CC, with CC from 00–99.
- `tape.usda`: red paper markings for the 390 × 430 mm scatter area and 104 mm
  square holder area. The thin visual tape does not create a physical barrier.

Masses, friction and the button spring are simulation approximations, not measured
product properties. The clock uses simulation time and pauses during API waits.
The pens, holder and timer are free physical objects; no grasp attachments or
table anchors are used. Robot parameters retain the upstream native settings.

The YAM robot, workstation and calibrated cameras come from `yam-arena`; its
dependencies and robot assets retain their upstream licenses. Runtime and recording
utilities adapt the MIT-licensed local thermometer task templates; their copyright
notice is retained in `LICENSE`.
