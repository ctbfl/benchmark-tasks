# Asset sources

The whac-a-mole device (`assets/device.usda`) is generated by `model.py` from
primitive meshes. No downloaded meshes, product photographs, logos, fonts or
textures are included.

The base is 220 × 260 × 40 mm with rubber feet. Four round buttons, 48 mm in
diameter, sit in a 2 × 2 grid (90 mm front-to-back, 140 mm side-to-side spacing).
Each button is a prismatic joint with 8 mm travel and a 400 N/m return spring.
A two-digit seven-segment display sits on an angled stand at the far edge.
Masses, friction and spring constants are approximations, not product measurements.

The YAM workstation and calibrated cameras are supplied by the separately
installed `yam-isaac-lab-arena` dependency; its assets are not copied here.
